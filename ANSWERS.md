# Answers

**Note on what I was working from:** every image is 63 pixels wide (48, 58, or 80 tall).
That is very small. I viewed each one directly, and also viewed 8–18x upscales
(nearest-neighbour for pixel structure, Lanczos for shape) of the ones I could not
read at native size. The route answers (over/under/around) are mostly solid because
they only depend on gross shape. The identification answers are limited by the
resolution, and I have said "guessing" or "cannot tell" where that bites — the
colours and silhouettes are clear, fine detail like badging or model is simply not
present in the file.

All images share a setting: a railway/road corridor seen from behind, with the
lane the runner is on picked out in a brighter colour.
