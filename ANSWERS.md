# Answers

Notes that apply to every image, stated once so I don't repeat them 22 times:

- **There is no HUD.** In none of these 22 frames is there a score, timer, lane
  indicator, distance readout, arrow prompt, swipe hint, warning icon or any
  other overlay. Everything I report is scenery drawn in the world.
- **The runner** (red cap, yellow headband, "26.2" bib) is in the MIDDLE lane in
  every single frame where he is visible. He never appears in the left or right
  lane. In several frames he is only partly visible, or missing entirely.
- **The "MILE n" signs** on overhead gantries are the only numbers anywhere. They
  count race miles. They tell me nothing about how far away the next obstacle is.
- **What distance cues actually exist:** perspective size, the repeating purple
  lane dashes and the dark horizontal road-slab seams (both regularly spaced, so
  countable), and a depth-of-field blur that kicks in on far objects. That's it.
  I use "slabs ahead" below to mean those horizontal seams.
- **Two recurring lane strips:** a dark-green mat with bright green streaks, and a
  dark-red mat with bright pink streaks. Each fills exactly one lane edge-to-edge.
  Neither carries any text, icon or arrow. I say what I see; where I guess at
  meaning I mark it as a guess.
- **The yellow hazard-striped beams** are the most common obstacle. They are
  raised beams on legs — the legs stand on lane lines or kerbs, so each beam
  covers a whole number of lanes and you can read off exactly which. The beam
  itself is up at around head height with open road underneath.
