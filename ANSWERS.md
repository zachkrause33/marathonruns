# A. Why OVER or UNDER, one sentence each

- **0916e7a5.png — UNDER:** The two yellow legs are planted on the road on either side of
  the lane and the striped panel hangs from the beam well above them, leaving an open,
  unobstructed gap of bare roadway between the legs at ground level.
- **11064634.png — OVER:** The heap sits low — it stops well short of the height of the
  lane's own width in the picture and has nothing above it and no opening through it — and
  the chevron arrows on the strip run straight into it, so the only way past is across the top.
- **2b41bc77.png — OVER:** The three cones are short — barely taller than the width of one
  of them — and they stand shoulder to shoulder across the full width of the chevron strip,
  so there is no gap between or under them, only above.
- **79eba93b.png — (AROUND, not over/under; see B note below)** — listed here only to say
  it is not an over/under case.
- **7bcc13c5.png — UNDER:** The striped panel and beam sit across the top of the frame while
  the two legs run all the way down to the road surface with plain, empty tarmac visible
  between them.
- **870e36c1.png — OVER:** The pipe stack is only two pipes deep in height, sits flat on the
  road with a plank across its top and no gap beneath it, and the yellow chevrons point
  directly into its face like a run-up.
- **88579957.png — UNDER:** The hazard panel is suspended high on the crossbeam and the
  frame's feet are bolted to the roadway at the lane edges, so the space at running height
  in the middle of the lane is completely clear.
- **907da895.png — OVER:** The barricade's top rail comes up only to about waist height on
  the two people standing directly behind it, and its solid striped panel fills the space
  down to a low bottom rail, so ducking under is out but hurdling the top is not.
- **c8f40c20.png — OVER:** Same read — the striped panel's top edge sits at roughly the
  waist of the marshals standing behind it, and the panel is solid from that edge down to a
  low rail near the ground, leaving the top as the only clear line.
- **e2137494.png — UNDER:** The striped board is fixed up under the crossbeam and both legs
  descend clear to the road, so the lane passes straight through the frame beneath the board.
- **e62ffea8.png — UNDER:** The obstruction is entirely the panel hanging from the top beam;
  below it, between the two legs, the road surface is visible and empty.
- **e9fcdedc.png — UNDER:** The striped panel occupies only the upper part of the opening and
  the tall yellow uprights frame an empty span of road below it.

Note on **79eba93b.png**: answered AROUND, not OVER or UNDER — the vehicle is a solid body
sitting on the road with wheels on the tarmac, taller than a person, with no gap underneath
