# Answers

Notes on how I looked at these, before the per-image answers.

The PNGs are 780x1688, which is what the viewer showed me, so my "normal size"
look really was normal size. Where I say something needed enlarging, I mean I
cropped that region and scaled it up 4-8x afterwards to confirm it.

Two things I worked out across the set rather than from any single image, and
which I lean on below:

* The road surface sometimes carries a **coloured strip filling exactly one
  lane**: dark green with bright green forward-pointing chevrons, or dark red
  with pale backward-looking streaks. Nothing in any image *says* what these do.
  Green-with-forward-chevrons reads as "go faster" and red reads as "slow down",
  but that is me reading the colours, not something the game told me.
* The **yellow-and-black hazard gantries** are legs plus a striped beam. In
  `1346b1e8` and `225917fb` the runner is standing *between* the legs with the
  striped beam above their head, so these look like things you duck or slide
  under rather than solid lane blockers. I can't prove that from a still, but I
  treat a gantry lane as less bad than a lane with a truck or a crate stack in
  it, and I flag it each time.

The mile signs ("MILE 13", "MILE 1", ...) tell me where I am in the race. They
are not distance-to-obstacle readouts, and I count them as "no" for question 3
every time.
