Here are some images. Each one shows a road ahead of you.

The road you are running on has exactly THREE LANES side by side, divided by
broken lane lines that run away from you into the distance. Call them LEFT,
MIDDLE and RIGHT as you see them in the picture.

Some pictures also show things beyond the edge of that road -- a kerb, a
pavement, a verge, a barrier, railings, water, buildings, or another road.
None of that is one of your three lanes. Find the three-lane road first, then
answer about it.

You are running down this road at speed and you must pick a lane.

For each image, answer four things:

  1. WHICH LANE do you take -- left, middle or right?
  2. WHAT DOES EACH LANE DEMAND? For each of the three lanes say one of:
     OVER (you must go over the thing in it), UNDER (you must go under it),
     AROUND (you cannot get over or under it, you must not be in that lane),
     or CLEAR (there is nothing in that lane).
  3. HOW SURE ARE YOU of your lane choice: sure, fairly sure, or guessing.
  4. WHAT DID YOU USE TO DECIDE? Say in your own words what in the picture
     told you. If something was unclear, say what.

If you genuinely cannot tell what a lane demands, write "cannot tell" for
that lane -- that is a real answer and it is more useful than a guess
dressed up as one.

Answer the images independently. Do not assume they are the same road, and
do not let one answer set up the next. Give your answer for every image, by
filename.

---- HOW TO WRITE YOUR ANSWER ----

Write a file called ANSWERS.json in this directory, and commit it. It must be
one JSON object whose keys are the image filenames, exactly as they appear
here. For every image:

  {
    "abcd1234.png": {
      "lane": "middle",
      "demands": { "left": "AROUND", "middle": "CLEAR", "right": "OVER" },
      "confidence": "sure",
      "reason": "in your own words, what in the picture told you"
    }
  }

"lane" is one of left, middle, right. Each of the three "demands" is one of
OVER, UNDER, AROUND, CLEAR or cannot tell. "confidence" is one of sure,
fairly sure, guessing. "reason" is free text and should be a real sentence or
two, not a label.

Include an entry for every image in this directory. Then also write your
reasoning out in full in a file called NOTES.md -- one short paragraph per
image is fine -- so that what you actually saw is on the record and not only
the four labels.
