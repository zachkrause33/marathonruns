How to play

Three lanes, one road, and everything on it is in your way. The controls take about five seconds to learn; the record takes rather longer.

Move

Left and right

Swipe or use the arrow keys to change lane. You can move as often as you like — choosing the right lane early is most of the game.

Clear

Jump and slide

Swipe up to jump a barrier, down to slide under one. Some obstacles can be ridden over the top of, if you find the ramp.

Cost

One contact ends it

Hit something unprotected and the record is gone for that run. Water and fruit along the road buy you a cushion — collect enough and a mistake costs you nothing.

The road decides your pace

Coloured mats are laid along the course. They are the only thing that changes your speed, and they are placed where the decision is hardest — beside an obstacle, on top of a lorry, across the line you were already committed to.

Green lifts you A short burst of pace. Worth going out of your way for — usually.
Red drags you A short loss. Sometimes the safe lane and the fast lane are not the same lane.

Today's marathon, then the tour

You get three attempts a day, and each is spent when you toe the line — a bad start is something to save, not something to throw away. Break 1:59:30 and the gold is yours to keep, and the remaining tries are yours too: chase a faster time, or tour another city. The next road opens at midnight Pacific Time.

Three

Tries a day

An attempt is spent at the gun, not at the tape, so every start counts. Gold for beating 1:59:30, silver for the city's course record, bronze for finishing the course.

Twenty-five

The featured city, then any city

Everyone runs the same featured city first — that is the race your friends are talking about. Finish it and your remaining tries open the tour: any of the twenty-five cities, working the whole map.

Kept

Streaks and record cities

Every city where you have broken the record is marked, and consecutive record days build a streak.

Twelve cities

Every course is set somewhere real, built from that city's own landmarks — the Colosseum, Tower Bridge, Table Mountain, the Skytree.

Six blocks, no spoilers

Your finish gives you a result you can paste anywhere. One block for each leg of the course: green if you ran it clean, yellow if something clipped you and your cushion took it, red if it cost you. It says how your run went, never where the obstacles are.
